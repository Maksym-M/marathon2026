Epic: Order Management

US name: Owner Views Consolidated Group Pre-order

Role: Customer (Owner)

Description: As a group owner, I want to see all submitted pre-orders of my group and the Grand Total on one screen, so that I don't have to collect everyone's choices manually and I know what the table will cost.

Pre-conditions:
- The Owner is logged in and has a reservation with a GroupSessionID (US1 from lesson). The guests count includes the Owner (e.g. "4 Guests" = the Owner + 3 Guests).
- Guests join the session via the invite link (US10 from lesson) and add dishes to their personal subcart (US11 from lesson).
- Guests submit their cart with [ Submit Pre-Order ]. A Guest's submitted pre-order goes to the Owner's group pre-order, not straight to the restaurant ("Your selections have been added to {OwnerFirstName}'s table."). Zero, some or all participants may have submitted.
- The Owner sends the group pre-order to the restaurant: the first time from the cart opened with [ Add Pre-Order ] (US20 AC5), later with [ Submit Pre-Order ] on this screen. The restaurant then confirms or rejects each dish. These flows are out of scope for now and will be added in a later release (see Assumptions).
- The Owner opens the "Group Pre-Order" screen with [ Group Pre-Order ] on a reservation card on the "Reservations" page (US20), or by its direct URL.
- Scope: this story covers the screen frame, the participant blocks with submitted items, the totals, access and reservation states. The "Dish Summary" section is in US22. Participants without a "Submitted" pre-order, "Not joined" placeholders, the progress line, the status badge, "Rejected" items, the cut-off, loading, "Last updated" and [ Refresh ] are in US23.

Acceptance Criteria (AC):
1. The screen must show the data of the one reservation it was opened for (the ReservationID from the card or the URL). Pre-orders from the Owner's other reservations must never be shown or counted (e.g. reservations A and B both have pre-orders - A's screen shows only A's participants and Grand Total). Opening another ReservationID (e.g. by editing the URL) must show only that reservation's data, with nothing left from the previous one.
2. The screen must show the title "Group Pre-Order" with the status badge next to it (US23 AC6), a [ ← Back to Reservations ] button above the title (it always opens the "Reservations" page, also when the screen was opened by direct URL, not the previous page in browser history) and a reservation summary line with Location, Date, Time and Guests in the format of the reservation card (e.g. "48 Rustaveli Avenue", "Oct 17, 2026", "12:00 - 14:00", "4 Guests"). Date and Time are shown in the restaurant's time zone (e.g.: UTC+4).
3. The "Participants" section must show one block per participant of the reservation's GroupSessionID. Participants are the Owner and every Guest who entered a display name and clicked [ Browse Menu ] (US10 from lesson); opening the invite link without entering a name doesn't add a participant (the seat is shown as a "Not joined" placeholder, US23). The Owner's block is always first, headed "My Pre-Order", whether or not the Owner has submitted. Guests with a "Submitted" pre-order follow, headed "Guest: {DisplayName}" and ordered by the time of their first submission (if equal, by the time they joined). Participants without a "Submitted" pre-order and seats nobody has joined are shown as defined in US23.
4. If two or more Guests have the same display name (compared case-insensitively, ignoring leading and trailing spaces), the Guest who joined first keeps the name and later ones get a number in join order: "Guest: Nino", "Guest: Nino (2)", "Guest: Nino (3)". Every joined Guest is counted, including those who haven't submitted, so a label never changes after a later submission. The Owner is not counted, because the Owner's block is always "My Pre-Order".
5. A participant's block must show only items from their "Submitted" pre-orders. A Guest's pre-order is "Submitted" once the Guest clicks [ Submit Pre-Order ], whether or not the Owner has sent it to the restaurant yet; the Owner's items are "Submitted" once the Owner has sent them. Items are merged into one line per dish (same MenuItemID): dish image (a circle with the first letter of the dish name if there is no image), "{Dish name}", the total quantity and the line price = Σ (UnitPrice × qty), followed by "Subtotal" = Σ counted line prices (e.g. Lobio Clay Pot × 1 at $8.00 and later × 1 at $9.00 → one line, qty 2, "$17.00"). Lines are ordered by dish name A–Z (case-insensitive). Items in a draft (unsubmitted) cart must never be shown or counted. The screen is read-only: editing (changing quantities, deleting dishes, removing Guests, re-sending to the restaurant) is out of scope for now and will be added in a later release (see Assumptions).
6. Submitted lines must use the dish name and UnitPrice saved at submission: a later menu price change, a dish set to "On Stop" or a dish removed from the menu must not change the line, the Subtotal or the Grand Total. A dish that is currently "On Stop" shows the label "On Stop" next to its name.
7. The screen must always show "Grand Total" = Σ of the shown Subtotals (dish prices only; "Rejected" lines are not counted, US23 AC5), with the helper text "Excludes taxes and service charges." All amounts are in $ with 2 decimals and a thousands separator (e.g. Subtotals $26.50 + $40.40 → "$66.90"; "$1,234.50"; nothing submitted → "$0.00").
8. If a user who is not logged in (including a Guest from the invite link) opens the screen, or the login session has expired when [ Refresh ] is clicked (US23), the system must show the "Sign In to Your Account" modal and no reservation or pre-order data (data already on the screen is hidden). After signing in, or creating an account from the modal ("Create an Account"), the access rules are applied again: the Owner returns to the same screen; any other account gets AC9. Closing the modal opens the "Main page".
9. If a logged-in Customer who is not the Owner opens the screen URL, or the ReservationID doesn't exist or isn't valid (e.g. "abc", "-1"), the system must show "You don't have access to this reservation." with [ Go to my Reservations ] (opens the "Reservations" page) and no reservation or pre-order data. The server must return HTTP 404 with the same response body in all these cases, so other Customers can't find out which reservations exist.
10. After AC8 and AC9 pass, the system must check the reservation state on opening and on every [ Refresh ] (US23), in this order (e.g. another Customer opening a cancelled reservation gets AC9, not 10d). The screen follows the reservation status ("Reserved", "In Progress", "Completed" or "Cancelled", US20 AC2):
   10a. "Reserved" and the Owner has sent the pre-order to the restaurant at least once → the screen as described in AC2–AC7 (in view mode after the cut-off, US23 AC7). If the guests count = 1, only the "My Pre-Order" block is shown, without the progress line and placeholders (US23).
   10b. "Reserved" and the Owner hasn't sent the pre-order yet (e.g. opened by URL) → the "not sent yet" state (US23 AC4).
   10c. "In Progress" or "Completed" → [ ← Back to Reservations ], the title and only the message "Your pre-order has been passed to the restaurant. For any changes, please ask your waiter."; no reservation summary, Participants, Dish Summary (US22) or Grand Total.
   10d. "Cancelled" → [ ← Back to Reservations ], the title and only the message "This reservation has been cancelled. Group pre-orders are no longer available."; no reservation summary, Participants, Dish Summary (US22) or Grand Total.

Assumptions / Open questions:
- Out of scope for now. These flows will be covered in a later release as separate user stories:
  - Owner places a pre-order: [ Add Pre-Order ] → menu → cart with the Guests' submitted pre-orders → sending to the restaurant.
  - Owner edits and re-sends the group pre-order: changing quantities, deleting dishes, resolving "Rejected" dishes.
  - Owner removes a Guest from the reservation.
  - Guest submits a pre-order and creates a new one.
  - Waiter reviews the group pre-order: confirms or rejects each dish and sends the result back to the Owner; the waiter's reservations list and the order during the visit.
  - Owner cancels a reservation; feedback after the visit (see US20).
- Assumption: [ Create New Pre-Order ] keeps the Guest as the same participant, so all their pre-orders are merged into one block. Each display-name entry via the invite link (US10 from lesson) creates a new participant. Open question (US10 from lesson): should re-opening the link in the same browser return the Guest to their existing participant instead of asking for a name again?
- Assumption: the number of participants is limited to the guests count, including the Owner (e.g. "4 Guests" = the Owner + 3 Guests), as the current build does ("Guest limit exceeded. Maximum capacity is 3, but 2 guests have already joined." from lesson demo). US10 from lesson doesn't define this yet; add the rule and the message a Guest sees when the table is full to US10 from lesson.
- Assumption: dishes have no options, modifiers or item notes (at least for now this is out of scope), so MenuItemID is the merge key. If they are added later, the merge key must include them.
- Open question: for a 1-guest reservation the card button still reads [ Group Pre-Order ]. Keep it or use [ My Pre-Order ]?
- Assumption: prices are in $ (this is what we see while website demo). UnitPrice has 2 decimals and qty is a whole number, so no rounding is needed.
- Dev note: all Subtotals and the Grand Total must come from the same data load, so AC7 holds even if someone submits while the screen is loading.
- Out of scope, follow-up: reservation location change. US1 from lesson says the invite link expires when the location changes, but no story or design lets the Owner change the location, and US1 from lesson doesn't say whether a new GroupSessionID is created. This needs an "Owner changes reservation location" story first, then a follow-up story for how this screen handles pre-orders made before the change.
- Open question (US10 from lesson): if the Owner opens their own invite link, are they recognised as the Owner rather than added as a new Guest? Recommended: yes.
- Open question (US10 from lesson): maximum display name length and how long names are shortened on this screen; are names made only of spaces allowed?
- Open question: non-functional requirements (mobile layout, accessibility, performance target, e.g. loads within 2 s for 20 participants with 50 items) aren't defined project-wide yet.
- Open question: is the Grand Total paid by the Owner, or does each participant pay for their own items? It doesn't change this view, but it decides whether a "split by participant" view is needed later.
- Out of scope: the flows listed above (later release), a participant leaving the session, reminders to participants, payment, access for restaurant staff (Waiter Flow), real-time updates (US23).
