Epic: Order Management
US name: Owner Tracks Group Pre-order Progress
Role: Customer (Owner)
Description: As a group owner, I want the consolidated group pre-order to show who has and hasn't submitted a pre-order yet, how many seats nobody has joined, and whether the restaurant has confirmed our dishes before the cut-off, so that I can remind the rest of the group and fix problems before we arrive.

Pre-conditions:
- The Owner has opened the "Group Pre-Order" screen for their reservation (US21).
- Guests can join the session by entering a display name (US10 from lesson); who counts as a participant is defined in US21 AC3.
- The restaurant confirms or rejects each sent dish (out of scope for now, will be covered in a later release, see US21).
- All cut-off times are counted from the reservation start time in the restaurant's time zone, using server time.

Acceptance Criteria (AC):
1. The "Participants" section must show one block per seat of the reservation, in this order: the Owner's "My Pre-Order" block first (US21 AC3), then Guests with a "Submitted" pre-order (order per US21 AC3), then Guests without one, in the order they joined, then one placeholder block for each seat nobody has joined yet. The number of placeholders = the guests count (which includes the Owner) − the number of participants (US21 AC3), or 0 if as many or more participants have joined (e.g. 4 Guests, the Owner + 2 Guests joined → 1 placeholder). The Owner is always a participant, even before placing their own pre-order. When a Guest joins, they take the place of one placeholder after [ Refresh ] (AC11).
2. Each block must show exactly one status: "Submitted" if the participant has at least one "Submitted" pre-order (US21 AC5); "Not submitted yet" if they joined but haven't submitted; "Not joined" for a placeholder. A placeholder is headed "Guest", because the name isn't known until someone joins. A block with "Not submitted yet" or "Not joined" shows no items and no Subtotal and doesn't count toward the Grand Total (US21).
3. The screen must show the progress line "{X} of {Y} submitted" with a progress bar, where Y = the guests count, or the number of participants if more have joined, and X = the number of participants with status "Submitted" (e.g. 4 Guests, the Owner + 2 Guests joined, all 3 submitted → "3 of 4 submitted"; nobody submitted → "0 of 4 submitted"). Placeholders count toward Y but never toward X. The reservation card shows the same values as "Pre-orders: {X}/{Y}" (US20 AC6). The progress line and placeholders are not shown when the guests count = 1.
4. If the reservation is "Reserved" and the Owner hasn't sent the pre-order to the restaurant yet (US21 AC10b), the screen must show [ ← Back to Reservations ], the title and "Your group pre-order hasn't been sent to the restaurant yet." with [ Add Pre-Order ] (US20 AC5); with guests count ≥ 2 it also shows the progress line (AC3) and [ Invite Guests ] (US20 AC6). Participant blocks, the Dish Summary (US22) and the Grand Total are not shown. After the cut-off (AC7) the message is "Less than 30 minutes before the reservation time. No changes can be applied." with no buttons.
5. A line the restaurant has rejected must show the label "Rejected" next to the dish name and "Qty: {n}" instead of the quantity, and must not be counted in the Subtotal, the Grand Total (US21 AC7) or the Dish Summary (US22). While at least one "Rejected" line exists, the screen must show the banner "Some items were rejected. Tell your guest and confirm their acceptance." The participant's block status (AC2) doesn't change.
6. The status badge next to the title (US21 AC2) must show exactly one value, checked in this order: "Created New Pre-Order" if at least one Guest has submitted since the Owner's last send, with the banner "{DisplayName} has filled their pre-order and is waiting for you to submit" (one line per such Guest); "Sent to Restaurant" if the restaurant hasn't reviewed every sent line yet; "Confirmed by Restaurant" if the restaurant has reviewed every sent line (some may be "Rejected", AC5).
7. From 30 minutes before the reservation start time, the pre-order is locked (cut-off): the screen must show the banner "Less than 30 minutes before the reservation time. No changes can be applied." and every line shows "Qty: {n}" without edit controls; the server must reject new Guest submissions; [ Add Pre-Order ] and [ Invite Guests ] are hidden on the card (US20). At this moment, every line that isn't confirmed by the restaurant ("Rejected", waiting for the restaurant's review, or a Guest pre-order the Owner hasn't sent) is cancelled and removed from the blocks, the totals and the Dish Summary; the participant statuses and the progress line keep their values.
8. From 45 minutes before the start until the cut-off (AC7), if the pre-order has lines that aren't confirmed by the restaurant (as in AC7), the system must show the banner "You have unconfirmed items in your pre-order. They will be canceled in 15 minutes." on the screen. The "Reservations" page (US20) must show a notification bar above the cards with "Reservation: #{ReservationNumber}" (a link that opens this screen) and the same text, one bar per affected reservation. The banner and the bar disappear when every line is confirmed, and at the cut-off.
9. A "Reserved" card on the "Reservations" page (US20) must show an orange dot next to the status when, since the Owner last opened this screen for that reservation, a Guest joined, a Guest submitted a pre-order, or the restaurant confirmed or rejected items. The dot disappears when the Owner opens the screen.
10. While the screen data loads on opening, the system must show a loading indicator and no data. If the data can't be loaded (e.g. server error, no connection, or no response within 10 seconds), the system must show "We couldn't load group pre-orders. Please try again." with [ Try Again ] and no partial data; [ Try Again ] repeats the load.
11. The screen must show the data as it was when it was loaded, with "Last updated: {HH:MM}" in the time zone of the Owner's device, and a [ Refresh ] button; no real-time push is required (same rule as US11 from lesson). While data is reloading, [ Refresh ] must be disabled and read "Refreshing…". After a successful [ Refresh ] or reopening the screen, the system must show the current data: the participant statuses and "Not joined" placeholders, Subtotals, the Grand Total (US21), the Dish Summary (US22), the progress line, the status badge, the banners and the lock are recalculated; access and the reservation state are checked again (US21 AC8–AC10); "Last updated" changes to the time of the reload.
12. If [ Refresh ] fails (e.g. no connection or no response within 10 seconds), the screen must keep the previously loaded data and "Last updated" time and show "Couldn't refresh. Please try again." under "Last updated". The message stays until the next successful [ Refresh ].

Assumptions / Open questions:
- Note: seats are shown as placeholders, not a "{N} guests haven't joined yet." hint, so that the progress never looks complete while a seat is still empty (e.g. "3 of 3" when the table is for 4).
- Assumption: "unconfirmed" lines (AC7, AC8) include lines still waiting for the restaurant's review. Confirm with the business: should the Owner lose dishes because the restaurant didn't review them in time?
- Assumption: "Rejected" lines aren't counted in totals, because the restaurant won't serve them; need to confirm.
- Open question: what does "confirm their acceptance" in the rejected-items banner mean for the Owner: deleting the rejected line and sending again (edit story), or a separate confirm action?
- Open question: the ReservationNumber format (e.g. "#48RUST1-101224-1030AM"). Define it in the reservation stories.
- Out of scope for now (will be covered in a later release; full list in US21): the waiter's review that sets "Confirmed by Restaurant" and "Rejected"; the Owner editing and re-sending, which resolves "Rejected" dishes and clears "Created New Pre-Order"; removing a Guest; what a Guest sees when they open the invite link or submit after the cut-off (US10 / US11 from lesson).
- Assumption: notifications are in-app only (no email or push).
- Assumption: the 10-second load timeout; need to confirm.
- Other shared assumptions are listed in US21.
